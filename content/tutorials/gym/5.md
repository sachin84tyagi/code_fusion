# Database Update Schedule & Architecture

To maintain the absolute highest degree of data accuracy in our Financial Intelligence Platform, the data ingestion engines must run on strictly defined cadences. 

Below is the architectural division of our database tables based on their required refresh frequency, complete with column-level explanations, practical data examples, and **Data Source Categorization** (differentiating Raw vs Auto-Calculated).

---

## 🧭 Data Source Categorization Summary
Before detailing the schemas, here is a high-level view of which tables are fed externally (Raw Data) versus generated internally by our Intelligence Engines (Auto-Calculated):

### 📥 Raw Data (Fed into System via Ingestion Engines / APIs)
* `companies` (Master Data)
* `securities` (Daily Price/Volume Data)
* `income_statements` (Quarterly/Yearly 10-Q & 10-K)
* `balance_sheets` (Quarterly/Yearly 10-Q & 10-K)
* `cash_flow_statements` (Quarterly/Yearly 10-Q & 10-K)

### ⚙️ Auto-Calculated (Generated by Intelligence Platform)
* `financial_metrics` (Derived ratios, TTM, Moving Averages)
* `valuations` (Calculated Intrinsic Value, Margin of Safety)
* `risk_profiles` (Calculated Volatility, Default Probability)
* `health_scores` / `growth_scores` / `quality_scores` (Engine Sub-Scores)
* `decision_profiles` (Final Engine Output)

### 👤 User-Driven (Input by User & Processed)
* `portfolios` & `portfolio_positions`
* `backtest_runs` & `backtest_results`
* `scenario_profiles`

---

## 📅 Daily (End of Day)
*These tables require daily processing to capture market fluctuations.*

### 1. `securities`
**Data Source:** 📥 RAW INGESTION
Contains the daily price action and market capitalization of the companies.
* **id** (String, PK): Unique identifier for the record (e.g., `AAPL_2026-07-13`).
* **company_id** (String, FK): Reference to the `companies` table.
* **date** (Date): The trading date.
* **close_price** (Float): The closing price of the stock.
* **volume** (Float): Total trading volume for the day.
* **market_cap** (Float): Current market capitalization (price * outstanding shares).
* **outstanding_shares** (Float): Total outstanding shares.
* **updated_at** (DateTime): Timestamp of when the record was inserted.

**Example Data:**
| id | company_id | date | close_price | volume | market_cap | outstanding_shares | updated_at |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `AAPL_2026-07-13` | `comp_AAPL` | `2026-07-13` | `195.50` | `45000000` | `3000000000000` | `15340000000` | `2026-07-13 17:05:00` |

### 2. `valuations`
**Data Source:** ⚙️ AUTO-CALCULATED
Daily intrinsic value recalculations. The margin of safety changes every day as the stock price fluctuates.
* **id** (String, PK): Unique identifier.
* **company_id** (String, FK): Reference to the company.
* **date** (Date): Evaluation date.
* **valuation_score** (Float): 0-100 score indicating valuation attractiveness.
* **intrinsic_value** (Float): The calculated true worth of the stock.
* **current_price** (Float): The price of the stock at evaluation time.
* **margin_of_safety** (Float): Percentage difference between price and intrinsic value.
* **valuation_method** (String): Method used (e.g., `DCF`, `Residual Income`).
* **is_undervalued** (Boolean): True if current_price < intrinsic_value.
* **generated_at** (DateTime): Timestamp of calculation.

**Example Data:**
| id | company_id | date | valuation_score | intrinsic_value | current_price | margin_of_safety | valuation_method | is_undervalued | generated_at |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `val_AAPL_260713` | `comp_AAPL` | `2026-07-13` | `85.5` | `230.00` | `195.50` | `15.0` | `DCF` | `True` | `2026-07-13 17:15:00` |

### 3. `risk_profiles`
**Data Source:** ⚙️ AUTO-CALCULATED
Daily recalculation of volatility scores and beta based on the newest closing prices.
* **id** (String, PK): Unique identifier.
* **company_id** (String, FK): Reference to the company.
* **date** (Date): Evaluation date.
* **risk_score** (Float): 0-100 score (higher means riskier).
* **volatility_score** (Float): Measure of historical price volatility.
* **debt_risk_score** (Float): Measure of solvency risk.
* **default_probability_score** (Float): Likelihood of financial distress (Altman Z-Score derivative).
* **risk_level** (String): Categorical level (e.g., `LOW`, `MEDIUM`, `HIGH`).
* **generated_at** (DateTime): Timestamp of calculation.

**Example Data:**
| id | company_id | date | risk_score | volatility_score | debt_risk_score | default_probability_score | risk_level | generated_at |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `risk_AAPL_260713`| `comp_AAPL` | `2026-07-13` | `25.0` | `30.0` | `15.0` | `5.0` | `LOW` | `2026-07-13 17:16:00` |

### 4. `decision_profiles`
**Data Source:** ⚙️ AUTO-CALCULATED
The final Investment Rating depends on the *current* margin of safety, meaning the decision engine must re-evaluate daily.
* **id** (String, PK): Unique identifier.
* **company_id** (String, FK): Reference to the company.
* **date** (Date): Evaluation date.
* **investment_rating** (String): `STRONG BUY`, `BUY`, `HOLD`, `SELL`, `STRONG SELL`.
* **conviction_score** (Float): 0-100 score indicating algorithm confidence.
* **rationale_json** (String/JSON): Serialized array of positive, negative, and warning factors.
* **generated_at** (DateTime): Timestamp of calculation.

**Example Data:**
| id | company_id | date | investment_rating | conviction_score | rationale_json | generated_at |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `dec_AAPL_260713` | `comp_AAPL` | `2026-07-13` | `BUY` | `82.5` | `{"positive": ["Strong Moat"], "negative": []}`| `2026-07-13 17:20:00` |

## 🗓️ Monthly
*Macro-economic data and certain fundamental adjustments occur monthly.*

### 1. `financial_metrics` (Monthly overrides)
**Data Source:** ⚙️ AUTO-CALCULATED
Certain blended metrics (e.g., TTM updates or moving averages) that don't need daily granularity.
* **id** (String, PK): Unique identifier.
* **company_id** (String, FK): Reference to the company.
* **metric_name** (String): Name of the metric (e.g., `200_Day_MA`).
* **metric_value** (Float): Calculated value.
* **fiscal_year** (Integer): Applicable fiscal year (can be null for pure time-series).
* **fiscal_period** (String): Applicable period (e.g., `TTM`).
* **calculated_at** (DateTime): Timestamp.

**Example Data:**
| id | company_id | metric_name | metric_value | fiscal_year | fiscal_period | calculated_at |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `met_AAPL_MA200` | `comp_AAPL` | `200_Day_MA` | `185.20` | `null` | `TTM` | `2026-07-01 00:00:00` |

## 📊 Quarterly
*This is the heaviest phase. Companies release 10-Q (Quarterly Reports), forcing ingestion of Raw Data and triggering Auto-Calculations downstream.*

### 1. `income_statements`
**Data Source:** 📥 RAW INGESTION
Quarterly revenue, gross profit, operating income, net income.
* **id** (String, PK): Unique identifier.
* **company_id** (String, FK): Reference to company.
* **fiscal_year** (Integer): e.g., `2026`.
* **fiscal_period** (String): e.g., `Q2`.
* **revenue** (Float): Total revenue.
* **gross_profit** (Float): Revenue minus COGS.
* **operating_income** (Float): EBIT.
* **net_income** (Float): Bottom line profit.
* **eps** (Float): Earnings per share.
* **updated_at** (DateTime): Ingestion timestamp.

**Example Data:**
| id | company_id | fiscal_year | fiscal_period | revenue | gross_profit | operating_income | net_income | eps | updated_at |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `inc_AAPL_26Q2` | `comp_AAPL` | `2026` | `Q2` | `90000000000` | `40000000000` | `25000000000` | `20000000000` | `1.30` | `2026-07-15` |

### 2. `balance_sheets`
**Data Source:** 📥 RAW INGESTION
Quarterly assets, liabilities, debt, equity.
* **id** (String, PK): Unique identifier.
* **company_id** (String, FK): Reference to company.
* **fiscal_year** (Integer): e.g., `2026`.
* **fiscal_period** (String): e.g., `Q2`.
* **total_assets** (Float): Total assets.
* **total_liabilities** (Float): Total liabilities.
* **total_equity** (Float): Shareholders' equity.
* **total_debt** (Float): Short + Long term debt.
* **cash_and_equivalents** (Float): Cash on hand.
* **updated_at** (DateTime): Ingestion timestamp.

**Example Data:**
| id | company_id | fiscal_year | fiscal_period | total_assets | total_liabilities | total_equity | total_debt | cash_and_equivalents | updated_at |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `bal_AAPL_26Q2` | `comp_AAPL` | `2026` | `Q2` | `350000000000` | `280000000000` | `70000000000` | `100000000000` | `60000000000` | `2026-07-15` |

### 3. `cash_flow_statements`
**Data Source:** 📥 RAW INGESTION
Quarterly operating, investing, and financing cash flows.
* **id** (String, PK): Unique identifier.
* **company_id** (String, FK): Reference to company.
* **fiscal_year** (Integer): e.g., `2026`.
* **fiscal_period** (String): e.g., `Q2`.
* **operating_cash_flow** (Float): Cash from operations.
* **investing_cash_flow** (Float): Cash used in investing.
* **financing_cash_flow** (Float): Cash from/used in financing.
* **free_cash_flow** (Float): OCF minus Capital Expenditures.
* **updated_at** (DateTime): Ingestion timestamp.

**Example Data:**
| id | company_id | fiscal_year | fiscal_period | operating_cash_flow | investing_cash_flow | financing_cash_flow | free_cash_flow | updated_at |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `cf_AAPL_26Q2` | `comp_AAPL` | `2026` | `Q2` | `25000000000` | `-5000000000` | `-20000000000` | `22000000000` | `2026-07-15` |

### 4. `health_scores` / `quality_scores` / `growth_scores`
**Data Source:** ⚙️ AUTO-CALCULATED
Intelligence scores based strictly on the raw quarterly financial reporting.
*(Showing Health Scores as the example, the others follow the exact same schema structure)*
* **id** (String, PK): Unique identifier.
* **company_id** (String, FK): Reference to company.
* **fiscal_year** (Integer): e.g., `2026`.
* **fiscal_period** (String): e.g., `Q2`.
* **health_score** (Float): 0-100 composite score.
* **profitability_score** (Float): Sub-score component.
* **solvency_score** (Float): Sub-score component.
* **liquidity_score** (Float): Sub-score component.
* **cash_generation_score** (Float): Sub-score component.
* **generated_at** (DateTime): Calculation timestamp.

**Example Data:**
| id | company_id | fiscal_year | fiscal_period | health_score | profitability_score | solvency_score | liquidity_score | cash_generation_score | generated_at |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `hlth_AAPL_26Q2`| `comp_AAPL` | `2026` | `Q2` | `92.5` | `95.0` | `88.0` | `87.0` | `100.0` | `2026-07-15` |

## 📅 Yearly
*Annual reports (10-K) and master reference data.*

### 1. `companies`
**Data Source:** 📥 RAW INGESTION
Security Master updates: Name changes, CEO changes, HQ location, sector classifications.
* **id** (String, PK): Unique company identifier.
* **ticker** (String): Stock symbol.
* **name** (String): Full corporate name.
* **exchange** (String): Trading exchange (e.g., `NASDAQ`).
* **sector** (String): General sector (e.g., `IT`).
* **industry** (String): Specific industry (e.g., `Consumer Electronics`).
* **description** (String): Corporate description.
* **currency** (String): Base reporting currency (e.g., `USD`).
* **updated_at** (DateTime): Last modification timestamp.

**Example Data:**
| id | ticker | name | exchange | sector | industry | description | currency | updated_at |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## ⚡ Ad-Hoc / User-Driven
*These tables are entirely divorced from chronological schedules and only update when a user takes action.*

### 1. `portfolios` & `portfolio_positions`
**Data Source:** 👤 USER-DRIVEN
Creation of portfolios and adjusting positions.
*(Showing portfolio_positions)*
* **id** (String, PK): Unique position ID.
* **portfolio_id** (String, FK): Reference to the portfolio.
* **company_id** (String, FK): Reference to the company.
* **shares** (Float): Number of shares held.
* **average_cost** (Float): Average buy-in price.
* **acquired_at** (DateTime): Timestamp of transaction.

**Example Data:**
| id | portfolio_id | company_id | shares | average_cost | acquired_at |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `pos_123` | `port_456` | `comp_AAPL` | `100.0` | `150.00` | `2026-06-01 10:30:00` |

### 2. `backtest_runs` & `backtest_results`
**Data Source:** ⚙️ AUTO-CALCULATED (Triggered by User)
Historical simulations generated by users.
*(Showing backtest_runs)*
* **id** (String, PK): Unique run ID.
* **name** (String): User-provided name for the test.
* **strategy_config** (String/JSON): The parameters of the strategy.
* **start_date** (Date): Simulation start.
* **end_date** (Date): Simulation end.
* **created_at** (DateTime): Execution timestamp.

**Example Data:**
| id | name | strategy_config | start_date | end_date | created_at |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `bt_999` | `Tech Value Test` | `{"sector": "IT", "min_health": 80}` | `2016-01-01` | `2026-01-01` | `2026-07-13 14:00:00` |

### 3. `scenario_profiles`
**Data Source:** ⚙️ AUTO-CALCULATED (Triggered by User)
Stress-testing assumptions.
* **id** (String, PK): Unique scenario ID.
* **company_id** (String, FK): Reference to company.
* **name** (String): Name of scenario.
* **parameters_json** (String/JSON): The "What-If" parameters (e.g., revenue drops).
* **output_json** (String/JSON): The resulting hypothetical valuation.
* **created_at** (DateTime): Execution timestamp.

**Example Data:**
| id | company_id | name | parameters_json | output_json | created_at |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `scen_1` | `comp_AAPL` | `Recession 2027` | `{"revenue_growth": -0.1}` | `{"intrinsic_value": 150.0}` | `2026-07-13 15:00:00` |

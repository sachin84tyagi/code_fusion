## 🚶 PART 4: WALKING & STANDING CAPACITY PROTOCOL

* **The Interval Walk:** Do not walk 1 km continuously. Split it! Walk **400 meters in the morning** (post-breakfast) and **400 meters in the evening** (post-snack). This prevents knee fatigue and exhaustion.
* **Kitchen Standing Trick:** While standing at the stove, never stay static. Shift your weight from your left leg to your right leg every 2 minutes, or rest one foot on a tiny 4-inch step stool to instantly take the load off your lower back.

## 🛌 PART 5: RECOVERY & MINDSET COACHING

* **The 15-Minute Sleep Rule:** To pull your sleep back from 1 AM to an optimal 11:15 PM, do it slowly. Sleep at 12:45 AM this week, 12:30 AM next week. Keep your phone out of the bedroom by 10:30 PM.
* **The "Do-Half" Rule for Pain Days:** If your joints hurt on any given day, **do not skip your routine**. Skip the lower body day, sit comfortably on your bed, and do 10 minutes of gentle arm curls and upper back stretches. Keep the habit alive!

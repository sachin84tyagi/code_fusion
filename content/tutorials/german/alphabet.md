## ⚠️ Mission Alert — The 3 Biggest Traps

These three letters trip up EVERY beginner. Memorize them now:

### Trap 1 — J sounds like Y

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Right |
|:--------|:--------|
| **J** = "jay" (English habit) | **J** = "yot" — sounds like "yes" |

> Example: **ja** (yes in German) = **yaa** — NOT "ja" like in English

### Trap 2 — V sounds like F

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Right |
|:--------|:--------|
| **V** = "vee" | **V** = "fau" — sounds like F |

> Example: **Vater** (father) = **FAH-ter** — NOT "VAH-ter"

### Trap 3 — W sounds like V

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Right |
|:--------|:--------|
| **W** = "double-you" / "wa" | **W** = "veh" — sounds like V |

> Example: **Wasser** (water) = **VAS-ser** — NOT "WAS-ser"

> 🧠 **Easy trick to remember:** German V and W **swap** compared to English. German V = English F. German W = English V. Simple swap!

## 📖 Part 2 — The 4 Special German Characters

Ye 4 characters sirf German mein hote hain. English keyboard pe nahi milte — but German speakers inhe use karte hain daily.

| Character | Name | Pronunciation | Memory Trick |
|:---------:|:-----|:-------------|:-------------|
| **Ä / ä** | A-Umlaut | Like "eh" in "bed" | A ke upar 2 dots = A thoda "eh" wala ho jaata hai |
| **Ö / ö** | O-Umlaut | Round lips jaise "O" bolne ke liye, fir "E" bolo | No Hindi/English match — practice needed |
| **Ü / ü** | U-Umlaut | Round lips jaise "U" bolne ke liye, fir "I" bolo | Like saying "ee" with pursed lips |
| **ß** | Eszett / Scharfes S | Sharp double-S sound — like "ss" | Ye sirf lowercase hota hai. Uppercase = **SS** |

### How to Type Special Characters (When Needed)

If you don't have a German keyboard:
- **ä** → type `ae`
- **ö** → type `oe`
- **ü** → type `ue`
- **ß** → type `ss`

> So **heißt** can also be written **heisst** — both are understood.

## 📖 Part 3 — How German Sounds Different from English

| Feature | English | German |
|:--------|:--------|:-------|
| Letters always sound the same? | ❌ No (e.g., "ough" = many sounds) | ✅ Yes! Once you know a letter's sound, it's always that sound |
| Silent letters? | Many (knight, write) | Very few |
| Letter C sounds like? | "s" or "k" randomly | Usually "ts" (Ce = tseh) |
| Letter S at start of word | "s" as in "sun" | Can sound like "z" as in "zoo" |

> 💡 **Big advantage of German:** Ye bahut **consistent** language hai. Ek baar letters ke sounds yaad kar lo — then **EVERY word is readable**. English jaise "ough" ka confusion nahi hoga! 🎉

## 📖 Part 4 — Grouping Letters by Sound Type

### Group A — Same as English (Easy Wins 🎉)

These sound almost exactly like English:

> **F, K, L, M, N, P, T, X** — same sounds, easy!

### Group B — Slightly Different (Watch Out ⚠️)

| Letter | German Sound | English Confusion |
|:-------|:-------------|:-----------------|
| **E** | always "eh" | In English, E has many sounds |
| **G** | always hard "g" (like "go") | English G can be soft (like "gem") |
| **S** | "z" sound at start of words | English S stays "s" |
| **R** | slightly guttural / rolled | English R is softer |

### Group C — Completely Different (Learn These First ⭐)

| Letter | German Sound |
|:-------|:-------------|
| **J** | "y" sound |
| **V** | "f" sound |
| **W** | "v" sound |
| **Z** | "ts" sound |

### Challenge C — Read This Word

*The security system displays a German word. Using what you now know, try to pronounce it:*

> **"zwei"**

**Breakdown:**
- **z** = "ts" sound
- **w** = "v" sound
- **ei** = "eye" sound (we'll cover this in Mission 002)

> This means **TWO** in German! You just read your first German number!

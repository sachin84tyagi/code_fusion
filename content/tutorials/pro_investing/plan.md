# 3. ABSOLUTE EVIDENCE RULE

Throughout the entire course, enforce this hierarchy:

## FACT

Directly supported by reliable data or official documentation.

## OBSERVATION

Something directly visible in price, volume, order-flow or market data.

## INFERENCE

A logical interpretation of an observation.

## HYPOTHESIS

A possible explanation that requires confirmation.

## NOT PROVEN

There is insufficient evidence to establish the claim.

## UNKNOWN

The available data cannot answer the question.

Never convert an inference into a fact.

Example:

Wrong:

> "Huge volume means FII buying."

Correct:

> "Huge volume indicates unusually high participation. The chart alone does not establish whether the participant was FII, DII, mutual fund, HNI, proprietary desk, market maker, algorithmic trader, or another participant."

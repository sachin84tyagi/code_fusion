## Chapter Overview

One of the most common reasons for a "perfect-looking setup" that fails is context mismatch. A trader sees an LPS pattern on the daily chart and enters a long — but the weekly chart is in a markdown. A trader sees an explosive SOS on the 15-minute chart and buys — but the daily chart is in Phase B with no directional bias. The setup was technically correct on its own timeframe but catastrophically wrong in context.

Multi-timeframe analysis is the solution: A structured, top-down approach that ensures you are always trading in the direction of the dominant trend across all relevant timeframes. The professional way to describe it is: **You use the weekly to identify the regime, the daily to identify the event, and the intraday to execute with precision.**

**The Core Rule:**

> **Every trade requires three alignments: (1) Weekly chart = Which side of the trade (long/short/neutral). (2) Daily chart = Which Wyckoff event (Spring/SOS/LPS/UTAD). (3) Intraday chart = When exactly to enter. When all three agree = 5-star signal. When any layer conflicts = Reduce conviction or skip. Never fight three timeframes.**

## LEVEL 1 — BEGINNER

### The 3-Layer Telescope Approach

![Multi-Timeframe Top-Down Analysis — The 3-Layer Telescope Approach](/images/pi-mtf-topdown-framework.jpg)

**Why three timeframes?**

```
THE TELESCOPE ANALOGY:
→ Weekly chart: Wide-angle lens. You see the entire mountain range.
   This tells you: Are we in a valley (accumulation), climbing (markup),
   at a peak (distribution), or descending (markdown)?
   
→ Daily chart: Medium lens. You see the specific mountain you are on.
   This tells you: Is there a ledge (LPS) to stand on? A cliff (Spring)?
   A summit push (SOS)?
   
→ Intraday (15-min): Microscope. You see the individual rock to step on.
   This tells you: Is this rock solid (positive delta, volume confirmation)?
   Can I safely place my foot (entry trigger)?

MISSING ANY LAYER:
→ Without weekly: You might buy an LPS in a weekly markdown.
   The LPS "holds" for 2 days, then resumes the weekly downtrend.
   You took a counter-trend trade without knowing it.
   
→ Without intraday: You identify the LPS correctly on daily but enter
   at the exact top of the intraday range (just before a small dip).
   Your stop is hit by intraday noise on a trade that would have been profitable.
   
→ Without daily: You see an exciting 15-minute breakout. But on the daily chart,
   this is a 5-minute noise move inside Phase B. Price reverses in the same session.
   You traded randomness, not structure.
```

### Layer 1 — The Weekly Chart (Regime Identification)

```
WEEKLY CHART RULES (NSE Nifty 50, Bank Nifty, Individual F&O Stocks):

WEEKLY SC IDENTIFICATION:
→ 1–3 consecutive weekly candles closing near their lows.
→ Volume on these weeks: 2–3× the prior 8-week average.
→ The final week of the SC: Often has a very long lower wick (intraday recovery from lows).
→ NSE historical SC candles on weekly: October 2008, March 2020, June 2022.
   Each was visible as a single extreme weekly candle.

WEEKLY PHASE B (Accumulation Range):
→ Range that spans multiple weeks (typically 8–26 weeks for major bottoms).
→ Weekly candles oscillating between range boundaries.
→ Volume declining week-by-week as Phase B progresses (normal and expected).
→ 200 EMA on weekly: Declining slope gradually flattening.
→ Nifty 2022 Phase B: Approximately June 2022 to November 2022 (26 weeks).

WEEKLY SPRING:
→ 1–2 weekly candles that pierce BELOW the weekly Phase B low.
→ On the SPRING WEEK: Candle closes significantly back inside the range
   (closes above the Phase B low).
→ Volume on Spring week: HIGHER than recent Phase B weeks.
→ This is the most significant single weekly candle of the entire accumulation cycle.

WEEKLY SOS:
→ A weekly candle that closes ABOVE the Creek (Phase B high / AR high).
→ Wide range. Closes near the high of the week.
→ Volume: 1.5–2× the prior 8-week average.
→ This is the "green light" weekly signal for the markup phase.

WEEKLY MARKUP IDENTIFICATION (Bias = Long Only):
→ Consecutive higher-high, higher-low weekly candles.
→ EMA alignment: 20 EMA > 50 EMA > 200 EMA (all stacked bullishly).
→ 200 EMA slope: Rising (not just flat).
→ Every weekly dip: Closes in the upper 50% of the week (long wicks at lows).
→ In this regime: EVERY DAILY PULLBACK IS A POTENTIAL LPS.
   You do not need elaborate confirmation — the weekly is your permission slip.

WEEKLY MARKDOWN IDENTIFICATION (Bias = Short Only):
→ Consecutive lower-high, lower-low weekly candles.
→ EMA alignment: 20 EMA < 50 EMA < 200 EMA (all stacked bearishly).
→ 200 EMA slope: Declining.
→ In this regime: DO NOT TAKE LONG TRADES regardless of how good a daily LPS looks.
   The weekly markdown will resume and stop you out on a temporary bounce.
   
THE WEEKLY BIAS RULE (non-negotiable):
→ Weekly Markup = Long only on daily + intraday.
→ Weekly Phase B = Neutral. Options selling. No directional bias.
→ Weekly Markdown = Short only (or cash, if uncomfortable with shorting).
→ Weekly Spring = Maximum alert. Begin monitoring for daily Spring + SOS confirmation.
```

### Layer 2 — The Daily Chart (Setup Identification)

```
DAILY CHART PURPOSE: Identify the SPECIFIC WYCKOFF EVENT that provides the trade entry.

DAILY CHART WYCKOFF EVENT LADDER (priority order for long trades):

1. SPRING (Highest Priority):
→ Daily price dips below SC low/Phase B low with low relative volume.
→ Closes back inside the range (reverse the SC low breach).
→ Volume: Significantly lower than SC volume (effort fails = CO absorbing).
→ Action: Begin monitoring for LPS immediately. Full scorecards for next session.

2. LPS (Best Repeatable Entry):
→ After the SOS, price pulls back on low volume.
→ Daily candles: Narrow range. Closes in upper half. Low relative volume.
→ Delivery %: 18–30% on LPS days (near-zero supply).
→ This is the MOST TRADEABLE daily event in the Wyckoff playbook.
→ Action: Wait for intraday confirmation to enter. Run full scorecard.

3. SOS (Momentum Entry):
→ Daily breakout above the Creek/AR high.
→ Wide-range candle. Closes near the high. Volume 2–3× average.
→ Delivery %: 60–80%+ on SOS day.
→ Action: Enter on first intraday pullback after SOS, not at the SOS close.
   (Buying at SOS close = Buying extended. Better: Wait for small pullback.)

4. TEST (Secondary Confirmation):
→ After AR or SOS: Price "tests" the breakout level with low volume.
→ Low volume test = Supply absent = Breakout genuine.
→ Action: Valid LPS entry when confirmed.

DAILY CHART DISQUALIFIERS (do NOT trade if present):
→ Daily Phase E (markdown): Consecutive lower-high, lower-low daily candles.
   Within a weekly markdown: Skip. Within a weekly markup: Monitor for LPS formation.
→ Daily SOW (Sign of Weakness): Wide-range down bar on above-average volume.
   This is the opposite of an SOS. Distribution signal on daily.
   If weekly markup and daily SOW: Timeframe conflict. Wait.
→ Daily UTAD: Price briefly exceeds prior high on low volume, then reverses.
   Short signal if weekly is in distribution. Long signal is completely invalid here.
```

### Layer 3 — Intraday (15-min/5-min) Precision Entry

```
NSE INTRADAY TIMING FRAMEWORK:

9:15–9:30 AM — THE OPENING RANGE (AVOID):
→ Do NOT initiate new positions in the first 15 minutes.
→ Why: Gap-up/gap-down from overnight news creates extreme volatility.
   Algorithms run stop-hunts in both directions during this period.
   Price often reverses completely within the first 5–10 minutes.
→ Professional rule: Let the opening range establish. Watch but do not act.

9:30–10:30 AM — FIRST HOUR (BEST ENTRY WINDOW):
→ By 9:30: Opening range is known. First directional impulse is visible.
→ VWAP is established (benchmark for the rest of the day).
→ Volume profile of the first hour tells you the intraday value area.
→ If the first hour shows a BULLISH SEQUENCE (higher highs on 15-min):
   AND this aligns with daily LPS + weekly markup:
   = ENTER on first pullback to VWAP or 15-min 20 EMA after 9:30 AM.
→ ENTRY TRIGGER: First green 15-min candle after the LPS pullback low,
  with positive cumulative delta AND volume > prior bar's volume.

10:30 AM–2:00 PM — MID-SESSION:
→ Trend continuation entries or LPS entries on mid-day dips.
→ If first hour established a bullish trend: Every 15-min pullback to VWAP = LPS opportunity.
→ Volume typically declining mid-session (lunch lull): Lower conviction entries.
   Use half position if entering in this window without a strong trigger.

2:00–3:15 PM — LATE SESSION:
→ FII programme trades often execute in this window.
→ Volume picks up as institutions close or adjust positions.
→ Trend is usually reinforced in this window (FII adds to winning direction).
→ If a bullish trend is established: 2:00–3:00 PM often sees a final push.
→ Look for: Volume increasing on final push + Delivery % picking up.

3:15–3:30 PM — NSE CLOSING AUCTION:
→ NSE UNIQUE FEATURE: The official closing price for equity on NSE is
  calculated as the WEIGHTED AVERAGE PRICE (VWAP) of the 3:00–3:30 PM window.
→ This means: The official NSE close is NOT the last traded price at 3:29 PM.
  It is the average of ALL transactions from 3:00 PM to 3:30 PM.
→ Implication for delivery %: Institutions that want their buy/sell to count
  for TODAY's delivery data will execute in the 3:00–3:30 window.
  High delivery % in the 3:15–3:30 closing period = Institutional commitment.
→ Professional rule: Do NOT exit winning positions during 3:15–3:30 unless
  forced (stop hit). Let the institutional closing trades work in your favour.
  The VWAP settle often improves your position vs the 3:15 level.
→ F&O SETTLEMENT: Futures and options also use 3:00–3:30 VWAP for daily MTM.

INTRADAY TIMEFRAME SELECTION:
→ 15-minute chart: PRIMARY intraday analysis tool. Filters out most noise.
   Each candle = 15 minutes of institutional order flow. Enough data per bar.
→ 5-minute chart: PRECISION ENTRY. Use only after 15-min has confirmed direction.
   Look for the 5-min trigger bar within the 15-min setup.
→ 1-minute chart: NOISE. Do not use for trading decisions. Too many false signals.
   Only acceptable use: Watching tape for last-second order flow before entry.
```

### The MTF Alignment Matrix

**The 6 alignment scenarios and their trading actions:**

**Scenario 1 — PERFECT BULL (5 stars):**

```
ALIGNMENT: Weekly = Markup + Daily = LPS + 15-min = Positive delta trigger

WHY THIS IS THE HIGHEST CONVICTION:
→ Weekly markup: Institutional consensus = uptrend. Every week, net buyers > sellers.
→ Daily LPS: The specific pullback entry that all Wyckoff traders identify as "the trade."
   Low volume, low delivery, near-zero delta on the pullback = Supply exhausted.
→ 15-min positive delta: The micro-level confirmation that buying pressure has returned.
   First positive cumulative delta bar on 15-min = Buyers stepped in. The LPS is confirmed.

COMBINED READING:
→ Weekly says: The regime is bullish. All major participants are net long.
→ Daily says: Today is the precise pullback entry point within that bullish regime.
→ Intraday says: Buyers are now active THIS MINUTE at this price.
= MAXIMUM PRECISION + MAXIMUM CONVICTION.

ACTION: FULL POSITION. 1% account risk.
Entry: First green 15-min candle with positive delta and volume confirmation.
Stop: Daily Spring low − 0.5 × daily ATR.
T1: Call wall level. T2: Next Volume Profile HVN.
Frequency: These align < 5% of trading days. Patience required.
```

**Scenario 5 — CONFLICT (1 star — Skip):**

```
ALIGNMENT: Weekly = Markup (bullish) + Daily = SOW / Distribution signal (bearish)

WHAT IS HAPPENING:
→ Weekly markup says: Institutional long-term bias is up.
→ Daily SOW says: Short-term selling pressure has appeared. Today was a weak session.

THE TWO POSSIBLE EXPLANATIONS:
(a) This is just a DEEP LPS: A temporary strong pullback within the weekly markup.
    The daily "SOW" will resolve upward in 1–3 sessions.
(b) This is the START OF A REVERSAL: The weekly markup is ending.
    The distribution is beginning at the daily level. Weekly will confirm this next week.

YOU CANNOT TELL WHICH IT IS TODAY:
→ If you buy (assuming deep LPS): You could be buying into a reversal.
→ If you sell (assuming reversal): You could be shorting the bottom of a pullback.
→ CORRECT ACTION: WAIT FOR WEEKLY CANDLE CONFIRMATION.
   After this week's weekly candle closes:
   If it is bullish (closes in upper half, low volume, maintains weekly structure): Scenario (a) — deep LPS. Enter next week.
   If it is bearish (wide range, closes near low, above-average volume): Scenario (b) — reversal. Prepare for distribution shorts.

LESSON: Missing a trade is not a loss. Entering a conflicted trade is.
```

### NSE Calendar Events — MTF Timing

```
EVENTS THAT OVERRIDE MTF ANALYSIS (temporarily):

RBI MONETARY POLICY MEETINGS (MPC) — 6 times per year:
→ Pre-event (1–3 days before): IV spikes. Intraday becomes noisy.
   AVOID: New directional entries 1 day before. The noise will misfire your entry.
   HOLD: Existing positions. Do not exit based on fear — wait for event.
→ Post-event (immediate reaction):
   First 15 minutes after RBI announcement: DO NOT TRADE. Maximum noise.
   Wait 30 minutes: Read the tape.
   After 30 minutes: The direction of the move is the institutional interpretation.
   If direction ALIGNS with your weekly/daily bias: ADD or HOLD.
   If direction CONTRADICTS: Reconsider position sizing.

UNION BUDGET (1 time per year — February 1):
→ Pre-budget: Often see a "Budget Rally" in January (anticipation buying).
   Delivery % is high in pre-budget rally (genuine buying, not speculation).
→ Budget day: MAXIMUM VOLATILITY. Do not trade intraday on Budget day unless
   you have a clearly defined post-budget entry protocol.
→ Post-budget (next session): The WEEKLY CLOSE after Budget week tells the real story.
   If weekly close is strong: Institutional approval.
   If weekly close is weak: Institutional disappointment. Potential Phase A beginning.

EARNINGS SEASON (Quarterly results — Jan, Apr, Jul, Oct):
→ For INDIVIDUAL STOCKS: Do not hold positions through results.
   Options IV spikes before results. IV crush after.
→ For INDEX: Results season confirms or denies the macro thesis.
   Strong FII buys during results season = SOS confirmation.
   FII selling during results season = Distribution or SOW.

FOMO EVENTS (IPO mania, sector themes, political events):
→ IPO frenzy periods: Retail capital locked in IPO subscriptions.
   Secondary market volume may fall (retail is busy with IPO).
   FII and DII data becomes MORE significant (retail absent = institutional dominant data).
→ Elections (General + State):
   Pre-election uncertainty: Phase B (range). Options IV elevated.
   Post-result (clear majority): SOS potential if FII confident in policy continuity.
   Post-result (hung parliament / unexpected result): SC risk.
```

```

→ Open Nifty 50 weekly chart.
→ Current phase: Markup / Phase B / Markdown?
→ 200 EMA weekly slope: Rising / Flat / Declining?
→ Weekly bias: LONG ONLY / NEUTRAL / SHORT ONLY?

STEP 2 — DAILY CHART (2 minutes):
→ Open Nifty 50 daily chart.
→ Current Wyckoff event: Spring / LPS / SOS / AR / Phase B / UTAD?
→ Daily event consistent with weekly bias? YES / NO.
   If NO: STOP. Timeframe conflict. No trade today.
→ Key levels: Spring low = ___. Creek = ___. T1 = ___. T2 = ___.
→ Daily delivery %: ___ (LPS: 15–30%. SOS: 60–80%).
→ Daily OI reading: Long Buildup / Short Covering / Short Buildup / Long Unwinding?

STEP 3 — INTRADAY (15-min, 1 minute):
→ Current intraday trend (since open): Bullish / Neutral / Bearish?
→ Consistent with daily event? If LPS on daily, is intraday showing stabilisation?
→ VWAP: Price above or below VWAP?
   Above VWAP = Buyers dominant intraday. Below = Sellers dominant.
→ Cumulative delta: Positive or negative? Turning positive (LPS trigger)?
→ Volume: Is this bar's volume confirming direction?

ENTRY TRIGGER IDENTIFICATION:
→ For LPS entry: First 15-min green candle with positive delta and volume pickup.
→ Price level: Approximate entry = current 15-min candle close.
→ Stop: Spring low (from daily) − 0.5 × ATR (from daily).
→ T1: Call Wall. T2: Next HVN from Volume Profile.
→ Position size: Account × 1% / (Entry − Stop × lot size).

TRADE RECORD:
Date: ___. Weekly Phase: ___. Daily Event: ___. Intraday Trigger: ___.
Entry: ___. Stop: ___. T1: ___. T2: ___.
MTF Alignment: Weekly ___/Daily ___/Intraday ___. Stars: ___/5.
Institutional Scorecard (all layers): ___/65. Conviction: Full/75%/50%.
Position: ___ lots.

POST-TRADE REVIEW (end of day or when closed):
→ Did MTF alignment remain intact? Did weekly/daily conflict emerge mid-trade?
→ What timeframe gave the exit signal (trailing stop, T1 hit, structure break)?
→ Was the entry timing (intraday trigger) precise? Any improvement?
→ Score the trade vs the protocol. Note any deviations.
```

## QUIZ ANSWERS

**A1.** 3-Layer Telescope: Weekly (Context): "Which side of the trade?" — Identifies the REGIME. Are we in accumulation, markup, distribution, or markdown? Sets the BIAS (long only / neutral / short only / maximum alert). Daily (Setup): "Which Wyckoff event?" — Identifies the SPECIFIC TRADE SETUP within the weekly context. SC, AR, Phase B, Spring, SOS, LPS, UTAD. Where you identify the trade. Intraday (Execution): "When exactly?" — Provides PRECISION TIMING for the entry identified on weekly + daily. The intraday confirms that the daily event is active right now (positive delta, volume confirmation, first trigger bar). Missing any layer: No Weekly = Counter-trend trades (entering LPS against weekly markdown). No Daily = Trading intraday noise inside Phase B with no directional basis. No Intraday = Buying at the daily high instead of the optimal intraday trigger = Poor entry, wide stop hit by normal noise. All three together = Right context + right event + right moment = Maximum precision and conviction.

**A2.** Weekly bias rules: Weekly Markup (Phase D/E): LONG ONLY on daily and intraday. Every daily pullback is a potential LPS. No short trades, regardless of how good a daily short setup looks. The weekly markup will resume. Weekly Phase B (Accumulation Range): NEUTRAL. No directional bias. Sell options premium (straddles/strangles between put and call walls). Wait for weekly Spring or SOS to confirm direction before taking directional trades. Weekly Markdown (Phase E): SHORT ONLY. No long trades, regardless of daily AR or bounce setup. The weekly markdown will resume and stop out daily longs. Weekly Spring (Phase C): MAXIMUM ALERT. The weekly chart has shown the most important reversal signal of the entire cycle. Begin monitoring daily and intraday for Spring confirmation and early LPS entries. The risk/reward at this point is the best of the entire cycle.

**A3.** NSE intraday time windows: 9:15–9:30 (Opening Range): HIGH VOLATILITY. DO NOT initiate new positions. Gap-up/gap-down from overnight news. Algorithmic stop-hunts in both directions. Institutional order books clearing. Wait for stabilisation. 9:30–10:30 (First Hour): BEST ENTRY WINDOW. Opening range established. First directional impulse visible. VWAP set. Volume profile developing. If first-hour trend aligns with daily + weekly: ENTRY WINDOW OPEN. Enter on first 15-min trigger (positive delta, volume). 10:30–2:00 (Mid-Session): Trend continuation or minor consolidation. Lower-conviction entries due to declining lunch-hour volume. Use 50–75% position if no clear trigger. 3:15–3:30 (Closing Auction): CRITICAL. NSE official closing price = VWAP of 3:00–3:30 PM. F&O settlement uses this price. Institutions execute programme trades in this window. Do NOT exit winning positions here. Let institutional closing trades work in your favour. If stop hit: Exit immediately regardless of time.

**A4.** NSE Closing Auction: NSE does NOT use the last traded price at 3:29 PM as the official close. Instead: NSE uses the Volume Weighted Average Price (VWAP) of the 3:00–3:30 PM window as the official closing price for all equity securities. F&O Impact: All futures MTM (daily mark-to-market) settlement uses this price. Option settlement also uses this. Delivery %: The 3:00–3:30 window is where institutions execute final delivery-based trades (real buy/sell, not intraday). High delivery % in closing window = Institutional commitment for the day. Professional rule: Do NOT exit winning positions during 3:15–3:30 unless the position is at a loss (reduce risk exposure). Institutional closing trades often PUSH price in the dominant direction = Winning positions improve in the closing window. Exit in the 3:15–3:30 window of a winning trade = Giving back gains that institutions would have added for you. The window closes at 3:30 PM sharp. Pre-open next session starts at 9:00 AM.

**A5.** Scenario 5 — CONFLICT: Weekly markup + Daily SOW: The daily shows wide-range down bar (2.4% drop, 1.8× volume, 62% delivery, large negative delta). This is a SOW signature on the daily. But the weekly is in markup (all EMAs bullishly aligned, weekly bias = long only). Two possible explanations: (a) Deep LPS: A temporary strong pullback within the weekly markup. The daily "SOW" is just an outsized LPS day. Price will recover in 1–3 sessions and resume the markup. (b) Genuine reversal start: The weekly markup is ending. The daily SOW is the first daily sign of distribution at the markdown beginning. The weekly will confirm this next week. Cannot distinguish today: If you buy (assuming deep LPS), you could be buying into a reversal. If you short (assuming distribution), you could be shorting against a strong markup. CORRECT ACTION: WAIT for this week's weekly candle to close. If weekly closes bullish (upper half, moderate volume, structure maintained): Deep LPS confirmed. Enter long next week. If weekly closes bearish (near weekly low, high volume, structure broken): Reversal beginning. Do NOT trade. Next week: Begin preparing for distribution short setups.

**A6.** Weekly Always Wins rule: When weekly and daily conflict on direction: SKIP the trade. The weekly is the DOMINANT TIMEFRAME. A daily counter-trend setup against the weekly = A low-probability trade. Override: NEVER override the weekly for a counter-trend daily signal. The only valid context for a short in a weekly markup is: When you have confirmed the weekly is ENDING (weekly SOW confirmed). Until then: Skip. Resolution process: Step 1: Identify the conflict (weekly markup + daily SOW). Step 2: WAIT for the weekly candle to close. Step 3: Evaluate the weekly close: (a) Bullish weekly close = Markup intact. Conflict resolved. Trade long next week at LPS. (b) Bearish weekly close = Markup ending. Now short setups are valid on daily + intraday. The resolution REQUIRES a weekly candle closure. You cannot resolve the conflict intraday or from daily data alone. The weekly is the final arbiter.

**A7.** Weekly SOS checklist — 5 criteria: (1) Weekly candle CLOSES ABOVE the Creek (Phase B high / AR high). This is the primary structural criterion. Price must close, not just trade temporarily above the Creek. (2) Closing price in the top 25% of the weekly range. A wide-range weekly candle closing near the LOW is NOT a bullish SOS — it is a failed breakout. Must close STRONG. (3) Volume above 8-week average. New buying entering the market = New buyers with conviction. Low-volume Creek breach = Lack of institutional participation = Suspect. (4) Weekly 20 EMA turning upward (from flat Phase B position). Short-term momentum shifting bullishly at the weekly level. (5) Weekly RSI: First reading above 55 since Phase B began. Momentum shift confirmation. Significance vs daily SOS: A weekly SOS represents MANY DAYS of institutional buying compressed into a single weekly candle. The weekly SOS = Institutions bought throughout that entire week's range. It is a much more powerful statement than a single-day daily SOS. One failed daily SOS attempt cannot cancel a weekly SOS. The weekly SOS is the most important bullish signal of the entire Wyckoff cycle.

**A8.** Intraday entry trigger for LPS trade — 3 simultaneous conditions: (1) First GREEN 15-min candle after the pullback low. Green = Candle closes ABOVE its open. This is the first signal that buyers have taken control of the current 15-minute period. (2) POSITIVE Cumulative Delta on that candle. The delta (aggressive buys minus aggressive sells) is positive for this bar. Buyers are not just lifting offers, they are overwhelming sellers. The institutional buying is happening NOW. (3) Volume higher than the previous bar's volume. The green bar has MORE participation than the prior declining bar. This rules out low-volume dead-cat bounces. The volume pickup confirms real buying interest. All three simultaneously: Green bar = Structure. Positive delta = Buyers dominant. Volume pickup = Conviction. Together: This is the highest-quality intraday trigger for an LPS entry. Without all three: It might be a noise bounce. With all three: It is the institutional buying pressure returning.

**A9.** Calendar events: RBI MPC (6×/year): Pre-event (1–3 days before): IV spikes. Avoid new directional entries 1 day before. Hold existing positions. Post-announcement (immediate): DO NOT TRADE for 30 minutes. Wait for noise to subside. After 30 min: Read the tape. Direction after initial 30-min reaction = TRUE institutional interpretation. If aligns with weekly/daily bias: HOLD or ADD. If contradicts: Reconsider position. Union Budget (Feb 1): Pre-budget: Often a "Budget Rally" in January (genuine buying, high delivery %). Budget day itself: MAXIMUM volatility. No intraday trading unless you have a specific post-budget protocol. Post-budget: Weekly close after Budget week = institutional verdict. Strong close = Approval. Weak close = Disappointment (potential Phase A beginning). Earnings season: Individual stocks = Do NOT hold through results (IV crush risk). For INDEX: FII buying pattern during earnings season confirms or denies macro thesis. Strong FII buying during results = SOS confirmation. FII selling = Distribution or SOW.

**A10.** 5-minute MTF check protocol: Step 1 — Weekly (2 minutes): Open weekly chart. Identify current Wyckoff phase (Markup/Phase B/Markdown). Check 200 EMA weekly slope (Rising/Flat/Declining). Determine weekly BIAS (Long Only/Neutral/Short Only). Note the bias before moving to daily. Step 2 — Daily (2 minutes): Open daily chart. Identify current Wyckoff event (Spring/LPS/SOS/AR/Phase B/UTAD). Check if daily event is CONSISTENT with weekly bias (if not: STOP, timeframe conflict, no trade). Define key levels: Spring low, Creek, T1 target, T2 target. Check delivery %, daily OI reading (Long Buildup / Short Covering / etc.), MACD/RSI confirmation. Step 3 — Intraday 15-min (1 minute): Identify current intraday trend direction. Check consistency with daily event (LPS on daily = intraday should be stabilising / turning). Check VWAP: Price above (intraday buyers) or below (intraday sellers). Check cumulative delta: Positive / negative / turning? Identify entry trigger: Has the first green bar + positive delta + volume pickup occurred? If yes: Entry now. If not: Monitor.

## KEY TAKEAWAYS

> **1. The 3-Layer Telescope: Weekly = Regime (which side). Daily = Event (which setup). Intraday = Timing (when to act). All three aligned = 5-star signal. Any conflict = Reduce conviction or skip. Missing a layer = Entering without full context.**

> **2. Weekly Always Wins. When weekly markup and daily SOW conflict: WAIT for the next weekly candle to resolve the conflict. Never counter-trade the weekly bias on a daily signal alone. The weekly takes 1–5 business days to confirm. Patience is the non-negotiable skill.**

> **3. NSE-specific timing: 9:15–9:30 AM = Never trade (opening noise). 9:30–10:30 AM = Best entry window. 3:15–3:30 PM = Never exit winning positions (VWAP settlement = institutional buying adds to your gains). Thursday Bank Nifty expiry = No entries before 11 AM.**

> **4. The intraday entry trigger requires ALL THREE simultaneously: First green 15-min candle + Positive cumulative delta + Volume above previous bar. Without all three, it is a noise bounce. With all three, it is institutional buying pressure returning at the LPS zone.**

> **5. 5-star signals (Perfect Bull: Weekly markup + Daily LPS + 15-min positive delta) occur < 5% of trading days. The professional skill is NOT finding trades — it is having the patience to wait for perfect alignment and then having the conviction to enter with full position size when it appears.**

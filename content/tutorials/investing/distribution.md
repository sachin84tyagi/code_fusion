# ⑭ Distribution — Tops Kaise Bante Hain

> **Distribution Accumulation ka exact mirror image hai. Jab professionals top pe apna stock retail investors ko bechte hain — tab distribution hoti hai.**

![Wyckoff Distribution — Complete Schematic BC to Markdown](/image/distribution_schematic_1789413552977.jpg)

# Distribution Kya Hota Hai?

## Simple Analogy

Tumne ₹100 mein property kharidi (Accumulation).
Ab value ₹500 ho gayi.

Tum yeh property ek saath nahi bech sakte — price crash ho jaayegi.

Tum kya karte ho?
```
1. Thoda thoda bechna shuru karo (weeks/months)
2. Public mein excitement banae rakho (bull market hype)
3. Jab woh greed mein khareedein, becho
4. Jab enough position exit ho jaaye, price gir jaane do
```

**Yahi professionals karte hain — Wyckoff Distribution.**

# Distribution vs Accumulation — Mirror Image

| Event | Accumulation | Distribution |
|---|---|---|
| Location | Bottom | Top |
| Who benefits | Buyers | Sellers |
| Context | After downtrend | After uptrend |
| Key climax | Selling Climax (SC) | Buying Climax (BC) |
| Key trap | Spring (below support) | Upthrust (above resistance) |
| Ending move | Markup | Markdown |

# Wyckoff Distribution Schematic

```
Price
│                     BC
│              ─────────────────────────────── (Resistance/BC high)
│             │    ───  ───  ───               │
│             │   │   ──   ──   ────   UTAD    │ SOW
│             │   │                ──          │  ─
│             │ AR│   (Trading Range)│ LPSY    │ ─
│             │   │                 ─────      │─
│             │   │                            │
│             └─── (AR low — bottom of range)  │
│                                              │ MARKDOWN
└──────────────────────────────────────────────
                                           Time
```

**Key Points:**
- BC = Buying Climax
- AR = Automatic Reaction
- ST = Secondary Test
- UTAD = Upthrust After Distribution
- SOW = Sign of Weakness
- LPSY = Last Point of Supply

# Har Phase Ki Detailed Explanation

## Phase A — Stopping the Uptrend

**Buying Climax (BC)**
```
- Market bahut chadha hua hai (extended uptrend)
- Ek day/week pe: Very high volume, wide spread up bar
- Everyone is excited — news is positive, media bullish
- Close near top (but reversal coming)
- Then immediate selloff (Automatic Reaction)

→ Euphoric buyers ne sab khareed liya. Demand temporarily exhausted.
→ Professionals quietly sold into this buying.
```

**Automatic Reaction (AR)**
```
- BC ke baad quick selloff
- Moderate-high volume, down bars
- Establishes the LOWER BOUNDARY of the range (support)

→ Professionals selling into lingering demand.
```

**Secondary Test (ST)**
```
- Price wapas BC area mein aati hai
- Volume BC se KAM hona chahiye
- Close usually below BC high

→ Testing demand: Still there? Usually less demand = Weakness sign.
```

---

## Phase B — Building the Cause (For Markdown)

```
Market sideways trade karta hai (weeks ya months)
Professionals slowly distribute karte hain

Multiple ups and downs:
- Tests of BC level (resistance)
- Tests of AR level (support)
- Some mini Springs (testing demand below support) — but they don't hold
- Upthrusts (distributing into breakout excitement)

Volume: Mixed but declining on up bars (bad sign — demand reducing)

→ "Building Cause" — More distribution = Bigger eventual decline.
```

---

## Phase C — Testing (UTAD)

**Upthrust After Distribution (UTAD)**
```
- Price briefly goes above BC high (above resistance)
- Retail traders excited: "All-time high! New breakout! BUY!"
- Professionals sell their remaining position into this excitement
- Quick reversal back below resistance

→ Last demand absorbed. Greedy holders trapped at the top.
```

**Note:** Not all distributions have a UTAD. Some go directly from Phase B to SOW.

---

## Phase D — Sign of Weakness

**Sign of Weakness (SOW)**
```
- After UTAD + Failed Test:
- Wide spread Down bar, High Volume, Close near Bottom
- Price breaks below the trading range support

→ Professionals are DONE distributing.
→ No more buyers — supply overwhelming.
→ "We have sold everything. Time to let it fall."
```

**Last Point of Supply (LPSY)**
```
- After SOW, price bounces slightly
- LOW demand on bounce (No Demand bars)
- Close near bottom on bounces

→ Last chance to sell before markdown.
→ Dead cat bounce — don't buy!
```

---

## Phase E — Markdown

```
- Price leaves trading range definitively (downward)
- Wide spread down bars, high volume
- Lower highs and lower lows begin
- No more distribution range

→ Accumulation will eventually happen at the bottom
```

# Complete Distribution Sequence

```
UPTREND
    ↓
BC (Buying Climax) — High vol, wide spread up, euphoria
    ↓
AR (Automatic Reaction) — Quick selloff, establishes range bottom
    ↓
ST (Secondary Test) — Lower volume retest of BC area
    ↓
PHASE B (Building Cause) — Weeks/months of distribution
    ↓
UTAD (Phase C) — Brief breakout above BC, quick reversal
    ↓
TEST of UTAD — Weak demand, close near bottom ❌
    ↓
SOW (Sign of Weakness) — Wide spread down, high volume, breaks range
    ↓
LPSY (Last Point of Supply) — Low volume dead cat bounce
    ↓
MARKDOWN BEGINS
```

# VSA Signals In Distribution

| Phase | VSA Signal | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| BC | Buying Climax | Demand exhausting |
| ST | No Demand bars | Less demand on tests |
| Phase B | Upthrust bars | Professionals selling |
| UTAD | Upthrust | Last demand trapped |
| Post-UTAD | Failed Test | Demand gone |
| SOW | Effort = Result (Down) | Professionals selling |
| LPSY | No Demand on bounce | Safe to sell/short |

---

# Key Warning Signs During Distribution

```
🚩 Wide spread up bars with high volume but close near bottom
   → Professionals selling into retail buying (Absorption of demand)

🚩 Up bars with decreasing volume approaching resistance
   → No Demand — buyers getting exhausted

🚩 Upthrust — price briefly above resistance then reversal
   → Bull trap — distribution peak

🚩 Wide spread down bar with high volume after resistance
   → SOW — distribution complete, markdown beginning

🚩 Small bounces with very low volume
   → Dead cat bounces — LPSY — do not buy
```

---

# Common Mistakes During Distribution

## Mistake 1: Buying The Upthrust (UTAD)

```
❌ "New all-time high! Breakout! BUY!"
✅ Check: Volume high? Spread wide? Close near bottom?
   If yes → UTAD → Do NOT buy → This is a trap
```

## Mistake 2: Buying LPSY Bounces

```
❌ "Stock giri bahut — bargain pe khareed lo!"
✅ Pehle check: Distribution range complete hua?
   SOW aayi? If yes → Do not buy bounces
   Wait for accumulation signs.
```

## Mistake 3: Ignoring Background

```
❌ Sirf aaj ka bar dekh ke decision lena
✅ 3-6 months ka chart dekho — distribution forming?
   Background mein weakness ke signs hain?
```

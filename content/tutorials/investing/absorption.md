# Absorption Kya Hota Hai?

## Simple Analogy

> **Absorption ek aise concept hai jahan price bahut kam chalti hai lekin volume bahut zyada hoti hai. Yahi woh moment hai jab "koi bahut bada" dusri side ko chup-chaap khareed/bech raha hota hai.**

![Absorption — Supply and Demand Absorption Side by Side](/image/absorption_volume_chart_1789413540061.jpg)

---

Socho ek fish tank mein pani bahut maila ho gaya (supply bahut zyada hai).

Ek bada filter machine aati hai aur dheere dheere saari gandgi absorb kar leti hai.

Bahar se dekhne mein pani same lagta hai, lekin andar sab kuch change ho raha hai.

**Market mein:**
```
Sellers (supply) bahut hain → Price neeche jaani chahiye
Lekin price neeche nahi ja rahi
Kyun? Professionals har sell order khareed rahe hain (absorb kar rahe hain)
```

---

# Absorption Ke 2 Types

## Demand Absorption (Bearish)

```
Market upar ja rahi hai (demand zyada lag rahi hai)
Lekin professionals retail buying mein quietly bech rahe hain
→ Demand absorb ho rahi hai
→ Yeh Distribution hai

Chart par:
- High volume
- Narrow spread on up bars
- Close near bottom on up bars
- Market barely moves up despite activity
```

## Supply Absorption (Bullish)

```
Market neeche ja rahi hai (supply zyada lag rahi hai)
Lekin professionals retail selling khareed rahe hain
→ Supply absorb ho rahi hai
→ Yeh Accumulation hai

Chart par:
- High volume
- Narrow spread on down bars
- Close near top on down bars
- Market barely moves down despite selling
```

# Absorption Ka Chart Par Appearance

## Supply Absorption (Accumulation mein)

```
Price: Slowly declining or sideways
Volume: High, especially on down bars

Chart pattern:
₹100 ────────────────────────────── Support
₹98  ──│──│──│──│── (multiple tests of support)
₹96  ──│── (never breaking significantly)

Volume:
████ ███ ████ ██ ████  ← High volume on every test
```

**Matlab:** Sellers bar baar supply laa rahe hain, buyers bar baar absorb kar rahe hain. Price zyada neeche nahi gayi → Supply being absorbed.

## Demand Absorption (Distribution mein)

```
Price: Slowly rising or sideways near top
Volume: High, especially on up bars

Chart pattern:
₹200 ─── ─── ─── (multiple tests of resistance)
₹202 ─── ─── (barely breaking above)

Volume:
████ ███ ████ ██ ████ ← High volume on every push up
```

**Matlab:** Buyers bar baar demand laa rahe hain, sellers bar baar absorb kar rahe hain. Price zyada upar nahi gayi → Demand being absorbed.

# Key Characteristics of Absorption

```
✅ High Volume (bahut activity)
✅ But LIMITED Price Movement (narrow range)
✅ Multiple tests of same level
✅ Close position changes on same level bars:
   - Supply Absorption: Down bars but closing near TOP
   - Demand Absorption: Up bars but closing near BOTTOM
```

---

# Effort vs Result — Absorption Connection

Absorption ek extended "Effort ≠ Result" situation hai:

```
Normal:
High Volume + Narrow Spread = 1 bar → Alert

Absorption:
High Volume + Narrow Spread = 5-10+ bars → CONFIRMATION
```

Jab yeh repeated hota rahe, woh absorption confirm karta hai.

---

# Absorption Kab Khatam Hota Hai?

## Supply Absorption Ends (Bullish)

```
Supply absorb ho gayi → No more sellers
→ Any small demand = price rapidly upar
→ Spring ya Shakeout → Then rapid markup
```

## Demand Absorption Ends (Bearish)

```
Demand absorb ho gayi → No more buyers
→ Any small supply = price rapidly neeche
→ Upthrust → Then rapid markdown
```

---

# Absorption vs Consolidation — Difference

**Consolidation (Normal Sideways):**
```
Volume: Low to medium
Spread: Normal
Price: Moving in a range
No clear absorption pattern
→ Market just resting — no strong signal
```

**Absorption:**
```
Volume: High and consistent
Spread: Narrow despite high volume
Price: Barely moving despite activity
→ SIGNAL: Major players accumulating or distributing
```

# Real Example — Stock Absorbing Supply

```
Day 1-3:  Market at ₹500 support, High volume, price ₹495-505 (narrow range)
Day 4-6:  More high volume, still ₹493-507 range
Day 7-9:  Volume continues high, ₹496-504 range
Day 10:   Price pushes below ₹500 (Spring!) — Low volume
Day 11:   Price comes back to ₹501 (recovery)
Day 12-15: Test of ₹500 — Very low volume, close near top
Day 16:   Wide spread up bar, high volume, close near top → MARKUP BEGINS!
```

Days 1-9 = Supply absorption happened.
Day 10 = Spring.
Days 12-15 = Successful test.
Day 16 = Sign of Strength → Ready!

---

# VSA's Stopping Volume = One-Bar Absorption

Earlier we learned Stopping Volume:

```
Down Bar + High Volume + Narrow Spread + Close Near Top = Stopping Volume
```

This is **one-bar absorption** — buyers absorbing all sellers in a single bar.

Absorption over multiple bars = Same concept, extended over time.

# Summary

| Concept | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Absorption | Professionals absorbing supply or demand |
| Supply Absorption | Professionals buying all the selling (Bullish) |
| Demand Absorption | Professionals selling into all the buying (Bearish) |
| Key Signs | High volume + narrow spread + consistent price |
| Ends With | Spring (supply absorbed) or Upthrust (demand absorbed) |

---

> **Key Rule: Jab bahut volume ke bawajood price nahi hil rahi — koi bahut bada player kuch kar raha hai. Woh absorb kar raha hai. Direction dekhne ke liye close position dekho.**

# Why Risk Management Is The Foundation

## Truth About Trading

```
Even the best traders:
- Win rate: 55-65% (not 90%+)
- Have losing streaks of 5-10 trades
- Sometimes make mistakes

Without risk management:
→ 10 losing trades = Account devastated or wiped

With risk management:
→ 10 losing trades = Manageable setback, recover normally
```

---

# The 1% Rule — Most Important Rule

## Definition

> **Never risk more than 1-2% of your total account on a single trade.**

```
Account = ₹1,00,000

1% rule: Max risk per trade = ₹1,000
2% rule: Max risk per trade = ₹2,000
```

## Why This Works

```
If you use 1% rule and have 10 consecutive losses:

₹1,00,000
→ Loss 1: -₹1,000 = ₹99,000
→ Loss 2: -₹990  = ₹98,010
→ Loss 3: -₹980  = ₹97,030
→ ...
→ After 10 losses: ≈ ₹90,438

You lost only ~9.5% — completely recoverable!

If you risked 10% per trade after 10 losses:
→ After 10 losses: Account nearly destroyed.
```

# Position Sizing — The Math

```
Formula:
Position Size = (Account × Risk%) / (Entry - Stop)

Example:
Account = ₹5,00,000
Risk per trade = 1% = ₹5,000
Entry = ₹1000
Stop = ₹950
Risk per share = ₹50

Position Size = ₹5,000 / ₹50 = 100 shares
Total invested = 100 × ₹1000 = ₹1,00,000 (20% of account)
Max loss = 100 × ₹50 = ₹5,000 = 1% ✅
```

**Key insight:** Position size depends on stop distance — not on how much you "believe" in the trade.

# R-Multiple System

## What Is R?

```
R = Risk unit (how much you risk on one trade)

If stop is ₹50 away and you have 100 shares:
R = ₹5,000

Win of 2R = ₹10,000 profit
Loss of 1R = ₹5,000 loss
```

## Why Use R Instead of Rupees/Percentage?

```
R standardizes everything across trades.

Trade A: ₹3,000 profit on a ₹2,000 risk = 1.5R
Trade B: ₹8,000 profit on a ₹4,000 risk = 2R

In rupees: Trade B looks better.
In R: Trade B is better AND we account for risk taken.
```

## Tracking in R

```
Trade 1: +2.5R
Trade 2: -1R
Trade 3: +3R
Trade 4: -1R
Trade 5: +2R

Total: +5.5R over 5 trades
Average: +1.1R per trade

This tells you your system's quality regardless of account size.
```

# Stop-Loss — Non-Negotiable

## Stop-Loss Kya Hota Hai?

> **Stop-Loss = Pre-determined exit point jahan aap maan lete hain ki trade galat hai.**

## Rules For Stop-Loss

```
Rule 1: ALWAYS set stop-loss BEFORE entering a trade.

Rule 2: Stop MUST be at a logical level:
   - Below Spring low (for Spring long trade)
   - Above Upthrust high (for Upthrust short trade)
   - Below last Higher Low (for uptrend pullback trade)

Rule 3: NEVER widen the stop after trade is entered.
   (If you do, you're changing 1R to 2R or 3R — destroying your math)

Rule 4: If stop is hit → Exit IMMEDIATELY. No hoping.

Rule 5: You CAN tighten/trail the stop (moving it in your favor).
```

# Common Risk Management Mistakes

## Mistake 1: Not Using Stop-Loss

```
"I'll manually exit if it goes against me."
Reality: Emotions prevent logical exit. Trade goes -50%.
→ ALWAYS use hard stop.
```

## Mistake 2: Moving Stop Wider

```
"My stop was ₹50. Price is now at ₹45. I'll move stop to ₹40."
→ This is called "hoping". 
→ Your original risk calculation is now meaningless.
→ Stop = Stop. Don't move it wider.
```

## Mistake 3: Averaging Down

```
"I bought at ₹100, it's now ₹80. Let me buy more — I'll average!"
→ You're adding to a LOSING trade.
→ VSA doesn't say this. Your setup was invalidated when price fell.
→ Exit the original position. Don't add.
```

## Mistake 4: Oversizing

```
"This is a sure shot trade! Let me put 50% of my account!"
→ Even great setups fail 30-40% of the time.
→ Oversizing = Emotional decision-making = Account destruction.
→ 1-2% rule. Always.
```

## Mistake 5: Revenge Trading After Loss

```
"That loss was unfair. Let me take another trade immediately to recover."
→ Emotional state = Poor decision quality.
→ After a loss: Step away. Review. No trade for rest of day.
```

# Drawdown Management

## What Is Drawdown?

```
Peak account value = ₹1,20,000
Current value = ₹1,05,000
Drawdown = ₹15,000 = 12.5%
```

## Drawdown Rules

```
If drawdown > 10%: Review system. Are you making mistakes?
If drawdown > 15%: Reduce position size by 50%
If drawdown > 20%: Stop trading. Paper trade for 2 weeks.
                   Review every recent loss. Find errors.
If drawdown > 25%: Complete break. Something fundamentally wrong.
```

## Recovering From Drawdown

```
Drawdown = 20% (₹1,00,000 → ₹80,000)

To recover 20% loss, you need 25% gain:
₹80,000 × 1.25 = ₹1,00,000

This is why:
- Prevention > Recovery
- Preserve capital above all
```

# Losing Streak — Mental Preparation

```
With 55% win rate, probability of 7 consecutive losses:
≈ 1.9% (approximately 1 in 50 runs of 7 trades)

With 100 trades per year:
→ Could happen once a year!

Pre-determine:
"I WILL have a 7-trade losing streak sometime. I am prepared for it.
My 1% risk rule means I'll lose 7% max. I can recover."
```

# Capital Preservation Philosophy

> **Pehla kaam hai survive karna. Profit second hai.**

```
3 principles:

1. Live to fight another day.
   A trader with ₹50,000 left can recover.
   A trader with ₹0 is done.

2. Risk of ruin > Risk of missing a trade.
   Missing one trade = Small opportunity cost.
   Blowing account = End of trading career.

3. Consistent small gains > Occasional big wins.
   +2R per week consistently = Wealthy trader.
   +10R one week, -8R next = Emotional rollercoaster.
```

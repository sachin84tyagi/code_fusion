# Upthrust Kya Hota Hai?

## Formal Definition

> **Upthrust = Ek deliberate breakout above a key resistance level that quickly reverses back below — trapping bulls and distributing supply.**

Spring ka mirror image hai:

| Feature | Spring | Upthrust |
|---|---|---|
| Location | Below Support | Above Resistance |
| Phase | Accumulation | Distribution |
| Purpose | Trap bears, gather last supply | Trap bulls, distribute supply |
| Signal | Strength (buying) | Weakness (selling) |
| Recovery | Quick up recovery | Quick down recovery |

# Upthrust Ki Kahani — Step by Step

```
Step 1: Market uptrend ke baad ek trading range mein hai.
        (Professionals quietly distribute kar rahe hain)

Step 2: Resistance level established hai.
        (Market baar baar yahan tak aaya aur ruka)

Step 3: Professionals price ko resistance ke upar uthate hain.
        (News, positive announcements, ya market manipulation)

Step 4: Retail traders excited ho jaate hain:
        "Resistance toot gaya! All-time high! BUY! BUY! BUY!"

Step 5: Professionals retail ki buying mein apna remaining stock bechte hain.
        (Distribution complete)

Step 6: Price rapidly wapas resistance ke neeche aati hai.
        (No buyers left — only trapped retail bulls)

Step 7: Market markdown begin karta hai.
        (Retail investors holding bags at the top)
```

# Upthrust — Bar Characteristics

## Classic Upthrust

```
✅ Wide spread bar (big range)
✅ Closes ABOVE resistance (briefly)
✅ Close near BOTTOM of the bar (sellers pushed back)
✅ HIGH VOLUME (professionals selling into retail buying)
✅ Quick reversal in next 1-3 bars back below resistance
```

**Strength of signal:**
- Close jitna neeche = Weakness utni zyada
- Volume jitna high = Indication utni strong
- Spread jitna bada = Upthrust utna potent

---

## Pseudo-Upthrust (P-UT)

```
Same visual — price goes above resistance and comes back
BUT: LOW VOLUME

→ Not as effective
→ "Still a sign of weakness" (VSA)
→ But weaker signal than classic upthrust
```

**Why Pseudo?**
- High volume = Professionals actively selling
- Low volume = Not much professional activity
- Low volume upthrust: Could be thin market, could be random noise

Still watch, but wait for more confirmation.

# VSA's Upthrust Rules (From The PDF)

**Rule 1: Close Ka Importance**
> "The lower the close, the stronger the indication of weakness."

```
Resistance: 100
High: 105
Close: 101 → Near bottom of bar → STRONG weakness signal
Close: 103 → Middle → Moderate weakness signal
Close: 104.5 → Near top → Markdown "not successful"
```

**Rule 2: Volume Ka Importance**
> "The higher the volume, the stronger the indication."
> "The wider the spread, the more potent the upthrust."

**Rule 3: Context — Where Did Upthrust Come From?**

> Normally after a wide range Up Bar with high volume into new high territory.

```
Context:
1. Market was in uptrend
2. Large wide spread up bar happened (possibly Buying Climax)
3. Resistance/high area reached
4. Then Upthrust happens
→ Much more powerful signal
```

# Upthrust Ke Baad — 5 Scenarios

VSA gives exact guidance on what to watch for next:

## Scenario 1: Next Bar = Down Bar, High Volume, Close Near Bottom

```
→ Weakness has set in and the trend is reversing
→ "Time to sell" (if volume is high)
```

## Scenario 2: Next Bar = Down Bar, Low Volume, Close Near Bottom

```
→ Weakness has set in
→ Wait for one more bar to confirm
```

## Scenario 3: Next Bar = Down Bar, Low Volume, Close Near Top

```
→ Weakness present but not immediately clear
→ Wait for next bar
```

## Scenario 4: Next Bar = Up Bar, Close Near Top

```
→ Weakness is NEGATED
→ Upthrust signal failed — possible genuine breakout
```

## Summary Table

| Next Bar | Volume | Close | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Down | High | Near Bottom | Sell — reversal confirmed |
| Down | Low | Near Bottom | Wait — likely weak |
| Down | Low | Near Top | Wait — see next bar |
| Up | Any | Near Top | Weakness negated |

# Upthrust vs False Breakout

**These are related but slightly different:**

**False Breakout:** Price goes above resistance and comes back.
**Upthrust (Wyckoff):** Deliberate move above resistance with high professional selling — specific context in distribution.

All Upthrusts are false breakouts, but not all false breakouts are Upthrusts.

# Identifying Upthrust on Charts — Checklist

```
✅ Step 1: Is there a distribution range (sideways market after uptrend)?
✅ Step 2: Is resistance level clearly defined (multiple touches)?
✅ Step 3: Did price go above resistance?
✅ Step 4: Was volume high on the breakout bar?
✅ Step 5: Did close come back below resistance or near bottom of bar?
✅ Step 6: Did price quickly reverse back below resistance?
✅ Step 7: Is the next bar confirming weakness?

If YES to most → Valid Upthrust → Weakness signal
```

# Stop-Loss — Upthrust Pe Short Trade

```
Upthrust high = Key level

Stop-loss usually:
- Above the upthrust high (2-3 points above)
- If price goes above the upthrust high again,
  it means buyers overcame — signal failed.

Entry: When next bar confirms weakness (close below resistance)
Stop: Above upthrust high
Target: Support of the distribution range (or lower)
```

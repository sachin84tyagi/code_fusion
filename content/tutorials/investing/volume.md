# ③ Volume — Market Ki Asli Kahani

> **Price batata hai KYA hua. Volume batata hai KITNI taakat se hua.**
> **Bina volume ke price action ek aadhi kahani hai.**

![Volume Types — Low, Average, High, Climactic](/image/volume_types_chart_1789314536173.jpg)

---

# Volume kya hota hai?

**Simple definition:**

> **Volume = Ek time period mein kitne shares/contracts trade hue**

```
Volume = 5,00,000 shares

Matlab: Is bar (ya candle) ke dauran 5 lakh shares haath badal gaye.
```

High volume → zyada participation
Low volume → kam participation

---

# Volume Kaun Create Karta Hai?

Market mein 2 type ke players hain:

## Retail (Chhote Log)
- Hum log — individual traders
- Ek trade mein 100-1000 shares
- Market pe koi asar nahi padta

## Professionals / Large Operators
- Mutual funds, hedge funds, FIIs, DIIs
- Ek trade mein lakhs-crores worth shares
- Inki activity se hi **volume spikes** aate hain

> **Important:** Bade moves mein hamesha bade players ka haath hota hai. Volume unhe chart par visible karta hai.

# High Volume vs Low Volume

Volume ko absolute numbers se nahi, **relative** terms mein padha jaata hai:

```
Average volume dekho (pichle 20-30 bars ka)

Agar current bar ka volume:
  > Average se 2x zyada = HIGH VOLUME
  = Average ke aas paas  = AVERAGE/NORMAL VOLUME
  < Average se kam       = LOW VOLUME
```

**Example:**
```
Last 20 bars average volume = 1,00,000

Bar 21: Volume = 3,00,000 → HIGH (3x average)
Bar 22: Volume = 95,000  → NORMAL
Bar 23: Volume = 30,000  → LOW
```

# Volume Kya Batata Hai — 4 Key Concepts

## 1. Volume = Interest / Participation

High volume → Bahut zyada interest (dono sides se — buyers AND sellers)
Low volume → Kam interest — market "meh" mood mein hai

## 2. Volume Direction Nahi Batata

Yeh common misconception hai:
```
❌ High volume = Bullish
❌ High volume = Bearish
✅ High volume = High participation (buyers ya sellers mein se koi jeet raha hai)
```

Direction Price batata hai. Volume participation batata hai.

## 3. Volume Ke Saath Price Ka Agreement

```
Price Up + High Volume   = Strong Up Move (buyers zyada hain)
Price Down + High Volume = Strong Down Move (sellers zyada hain)
Price Up + Low Volume    = Weak Up Move (buyers kam hain) → No Demand
Price Down + Low Volume  = Weak Down Move (sellers kam hain) → No Supply
```

## 4. Climactic Volume

Jab volume suddenly bahut zyada ho jaaye (4x-10x average se bhi zyada):

```
Normal volume: 1,00,000
Climactic volume: 8,00,000-15,00,000
```

Yeh market mein ek turning point signal kar sakta hai — panic selling ya euphoric buying.

# Practical Example — Nifty Scenario

```
Date     | Price | Volume   | Reading
---------|-------|----------|---------------------------
Sep 1    | 25000 | 1.2Cr    | Normal
Sep 2    | 25200 | 1.5Cr    | Normal up move
Sep 3    | 25150 | 0.4Cr    | LOW volume — weak selling (No Supply)
Sep 4    | 25400 | 3.8Cr    | HIGH volume up — strong buying!
Sep 5    | 25380 | 0.3Cr    | LOW volume — buyers taking rest
Sep 6    | 25100 | 6.5Cr    | CLIMACTIC — panic selling?
```

Sep 6 pe 6.5 crore volume ek warning hai. Buyers aur sellers dono mein se koi badi force active ho gayi.

# VSA Mein Volume Ka Rule

VSA kehta hai:

> **Volume = Effort (Effort kitna laga)**
> **Spread = Result (Kya result nikla)**

Jab Effort aur Result match karte hain → Normal
Jab Effort aur Result match NAHI karte → **Signal!**

```
HIGH effort (High Volume) + SMALL result (Narrow Spread)
→ Koi badi force price ko rok rahi hai
→ Yeh VSA ka MOST IMPORTANT signal hai
```

# Volume Ke 5 Situations

| Situation | Price | Volume | Spread | Matlab |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Strong Buy | Up | High | Wide | Buyers clearly jeet rahe |
| Weak Buy | Up | Low | Narrow | Buyers weak hain (No Demand) |
| Strong Sell | Down | High | Wide | Sellers clearly jeet rahe |
| Weak Sell | Down | Low | Narrow | Sellers weak hain (No Supply) |
| Climax | Any | Very High | Wide | Turning point possible |

---

# Kya Volume Lag Hota Hai?

Ek important technical point:

**Volume real-time confirm kar pana mushkil hai** — especially intraday mein partial bars mein.

VSA mein hum usually **closed bars** ka volume use karte hain — matlab bar complete hone ke baad.

---

# Summary

| Volume Type | Matlab |
|---|---|
| High | Zyada participation — badi force active |
| Low | Kam participation — koi interest nahi |
| Climactic | Extreme — possible reversal |
| Average | Normal market activity |

> **Key Rule:** Volume price ke saath padhein, akele nahi. "High volume" ka matlab context ke bina kuch nahi.
